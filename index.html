How it works

A refined pipeline from 2D source to immersive 3D output

Prepare and enhance your video first, generate depth, optionally blend maps from different models, then render your final 3D output.

01

Upscale & Enhancement

Load your source video and upscale or enhance it first, before generating depth maps.

02

Depth Generation

Generate a depth map from the prepared video using your chosen AI model.

03

Depth Blending (Optional)

For a blended result, generate a second depth map from the same prepared video using a different model. Combine the two maps in Depth Blender before stereo generation. Skip this step to use a single map.

04

Final 3D Output

Use the prepared video with your selected or blended depth map to generate left and right eye views, adjust the stereo settings, and export your final 3D video.

FAQ

Answers for creators, collectors, and power users

A few of the most common questions around performance, compatibility, licensing, and workflow flexibility.

Does VisionDepth3D run on AMD or Apple GPUs?

VisionDepth3D can run on CPU across supported environments, but GPU acceleration is currently centered on NVIDIA through CUDA and ONNX-oriented workflows. Alternative backends may be possible where supported, while broader Apple Silicon and expanded GPU options remain future-facing development targets.

Can I use my own depth model?

Yes. Advanced users can add compatible model weights and integrate supported depth workflows depending on the model format. Both PyTorch and ONNX-oriented paths are part of the broader design philosophy for flexible local experimentation.

Is VisionDepth3D Pro a subscription?

No. VisionDepth3D Pro is a one-time $59.99 USD license purchase with no recurring subscription fee. The license key unlocks Pro features in the same desktop application used by the Free tier.

What is included in VisionDepth3D Free?

Free includes the core single-video and single-image 3D workflow, depth generation, Half-SBS, Full-SBS, Anaglyph output, basic guided controls, basic depth tools, frame extraction, and RIFE interpolation up to 2×. Free video renders are limited to 3 minutes, output height is limited to 1080p, and exports include a VisionDepth3D watermark.

What does upgrading to Pro unlock?

Pro removes the Free-tier video-length, output-height, and watermark limits. It also unlocks batch workflows, advanced keyframes, scene detection, advanced stereo and encoding controls, VR and VR180 output, passive interlaced output, Live 3D, full Depth Blender workflows, Real-ESRGAN, higher FPS multipliers, threaded processing, the job queue, and expanded official model access.

Can I use VisionDepth3D Pro commercially?

Pro includes individual professional and commercial use of VisionDepth3D software features and rendered outputs, provided you have the necessary rights to the input media and comply with all third-party model, library, tool, and media licenses. Team, studio, company-wide, hosted-service, resale, redistribution, and custom business use may require written permission or a separate agreement.

Where can I get support or follow development?

Support and project visibility are available through GitHub, the Itch.io page, and related project channels. Those platforms are the best places to track updates, releases, and issue reporting.
